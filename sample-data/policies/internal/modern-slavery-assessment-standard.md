# Modern Slavery Assessment Standard

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Classification:** Internal Policy  
**Authority:** Compliance & Legal

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This policy defines when modern slavery assessments are required, which suppliers must provide Modern Slavery Statements, and the decision criteria for accepting or escalating modern slavery risk. It ensures alignment with the Modern Slavery Act 2018 (Cth) and organizational risk tolerance.

---

## 2. APPLICABILITY

**All suppliers are assessed for modern slavery risk**, but assessment intensity varies by:
- Supplier annual turnover (triggers statement requirement)
- Industry classification (higher-risk sectors)
- Geographic presence (higher-risk countries)
- Supply chain complexity (global vs. domestic)

---

## 3. ASSESSMENT TRIGGERS

### 3.1 Legal Threshold: Modern Slavery Statement

**Supplier MUST provide Modern Slavery Statement if:**
- Supplier has annual Australian turnover ≥ $100 million, OR
- Supplier is a reporting entity under Modern Slavery Act 2018 (Cth)

**Statement Requirements:**
- [ ] Statement is current (issued within last 2 years)
- [ ] Statement published on supplier's public website
- [ ] Statement covers: risks, policies, remediation processes
- [ ] Statement addresses: forced labor, child labor, debt bondage, trafficking

**Assessment Outcome:**
- Statement present & adequate → Document in assessment, proceed to content review
- Statement absent & required → **ESCALATE** (regulatory non-compliance)
- Statement absent & not required → Continue with alternative risk assessment

---

### 3.2 Risk-Based Assessment Triggers

**Assess modern slavery risk for ALL suppliers. Trigger enhanced assessment if ANY apply:**

| Trigger | Risk Level | Assessment Required |
|---------|------------|-------------------|
| Supplier has annual turnover ≥ $50M (AUD) | MEDIUM | Yes |
| Supplier has supply chain presence in high-risk countries (see list below) | MEDIUM-HIGH | Yes |
| Supplier operates in high-risk industry (see list below) | MEDIUM | Yes |
| Supplier has >100 employees in overseas locations | MEDIUM | Yes |
| Supplier's supply chain involves manufacturing, agriculture, textiles, mining | HIGH | Enhanced |
| Supplier has <2 years trading history | MEDIUM | Yes |
| Prior negative media/regulatory findings re: labor practices | HIGH | Escalate |

**High-Risk Countries (modern slavery risk):**
China, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Philippines, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Mexico, Brazil, Philippines, Nigeria, Kenya

**High-Risk Industries:**
- Manufacturing
- Agriculture & food production
- Textiles & apparel
- Mining & extraction
- Construction
- Domestic work
- Wholesale & retail (high-volume suppliers)

---

## 4. ASSESSMENT PROCESS

### Step 1: Statement Review (if required)
**Responsible:** Compliance analyst  
**Input:** Supplier's published Modern Slavery Statement

**Questions to answer:**
1. [ ] Is the statement current (issued within 2 years)?
2. [ ] Does it cover forced labor, child labor, debt bondage, trafficking?
3. [ ] Does it describe supplier's supply chain (not just operations)?
4. [ ] Does it address risks? (Does it identify where risks exist?)
5. [ ] Does it describe remediation/grievance processes?
6. [ ] Is there evidence of due diligence on supply chain partners?

**Scoring:**
- All YES = Statement adequate (move to Step 2)
- Any NO = Statement inadequate (escalate for supplier remediation request)

---

### Step 2: Risk Profile Assessment (all suppliers)
**Responsible:** Compliance analyst + AI document intelligence  
**Input:** Supplier documents (annual report, financial statements, governance documents)

**Assessment Categories:**

#### A. Supply Chain Transparency
**Evidence to assess:**
- Does supplier disclose their supply chain publicly?
- Can they name tier-1 suppliers?
- Do they have supply chain maps?

**Decision:**
- High transparency → LOW risk factor
- Partial transparency → MEDIUM risk factor
- No transparency → HIGH risk factor

#### B. Labor Practices & Certifications
**Evidence to assess:**
- [ ] ISO 45001 (occupational health/safety)
- [ ] SEDEX membership or equivalent audit program
- [ ] Fair trade or ethical certifications
- [ ] Industry association compliance (e.g., AAFEI for recruitment)

**Decision:**
- 2+ certifications → LOW risk factor
- 1 certification → MEDIUM risk factor
- No certifications → HIGH risk factor (if high-risk industry)

#### C. Governance & Oversight
**Evidence to assess:**
- [ ] Documented labor practices policy
- [ ] Grievance mechanism for workers
- [ ] Regular labor practice audits
- [ ] Code of conduct covering modern slavery

**Decision:**
- All present → LOW risk factor
- 2-3 present → MEDIUM risk factor
- <2 present → HIGH risk factor

#### D. Geographic Risk
**Evidence to assess:**
- Where is supplier headquartered?
- Where do they manufacture/source?
- Presence in high-risk countries?

**Decision:**
- Domestic only (AU/NZ) → LOW risk factor
- Developed countries only → MEDIUM risk factor
- Presence in high-risk countries → HIGH risk factor

#### E. Financial Health & Stability
**Evidence to assess:**
- [ ] Positive working capital?
- [ ] Stable/growing revenue?
- [ ] Not in financial distress?

**Why relevant:** Financial pressure increases incentive to cut corners on labor practices

**Decision:**
- Strong financials → LOW risk factor
- Adequate financials → MEDIUM risk factor
- Weak financials/distress → HIGH risk factor

---

### Step 3: Risk Scoring
**Responsible:** Compliance analyst  
**Method:** Sum risk factors from Step 2

| Risk Factor | Points |
|------------|--------|
| Supply Chain Transparency: LOW | 0 |
| Supply Chain Transparency: MEDIUM | 1 |
| Supply Chain Transparency: HIGH | 3 |
| Labor Certifications: LOW | 0 |
| Labor Certifications: MEDIUM | 1 |
| Labor Certifications: HIGH | 2 |
| Governance: LOW | 0 |
| Governance: MEDIUM | 1 |
| Governance: HIGH | 2 |
| Geographic Risk: LOW | 0 |
| Geographic Risk: MEDIUM | 1 |
| Geographic Risk: HIGH | 3 |
| Financial Health: LOW | 0 |
| Financial Health: MEDIUM | 1 |
| Financial Health: HIGH | 2 |

**Total Score → Risk Classification:**
- 0-2 points = **LOW modern slavery risk**
- 3-6 points = **MEDIUM modern slavery risk**
- 7+ points = **HIGH modern slavery risk**

---

## 5. DECISION FRAMEWORK

### LOW Modern Slavery Risk
**Outcome:** APPROVED  
**Requirements:**
- [ ] Risk score 0-2 points
- [ ] Statement adequate (if required)
- [ ] No prior labor violations

**Actions:**
- Document assessment in case file
- Include in supplier master record
- Standard monitoring (annual re-assessment)

**Example:** Domestic services company with 50 employees, ISO 45001 certified, strong financials, transparent operations

---

### MEDIUM Modern Slavery Risk
**Outcome:** APPROVED with conditions  
**Requirements:**
- [ ] Risk score 3-6 points
- [ ] Conditions documented and agreed
- [ ] Supplier accepts monitoring

**Mandatory Conditions:**
1. [ ] Supplier must provide annual Modern Slavery Statement (even if <$100M turnover)
2. [ ] Supplier must commit to corrective actions for identified gaps
3. [ ] Enhanced monitoring: review every 12 months (vs. 24 months for LOW risk)
4. [ ] Right to audit: organization retains right to conduct labor practice audit

**Optional Conditions** (based on specific risk):
- Restrict spend to <$X per year
- Require third-party labor practice audit
- Require quarterly compliance reporting
- Restrict to lower-risk geographies
- Require supplier to remediate specific gaps (e.g., obtain ISO 45001 within 12 months)

**Example:** International manufacturing supplier with supply chain in mid-risk countries, developing governance framework, financials adequate

---

### HIGH Modern Slavery Risk
**Outcome:** ESCALATE to Compliance Manager  
**Requirements:**
- [ ] Risk score 7+ points, OR
- [ ] Prior labor violations/negative findings, OR
- [ ] Statement inadequate with high-risk profile

**Escalation Process:**
1. Compliance Manager reviews assessment + supplier background
2. Determine: Can risk be mitigated? Or is supplier unsuitable?

**Decision Options:**
- **REJECT** — Supplier poses unacceptable risk, cannot be engaged
- **CONDITIONAL APPROVAL** — Approve only with intensive risk mitigation (see below)
- **ESCALATE TO EXECUTIVE** — If business case is strong, escalate to CFO/COO for risk acceptance decision

**Intensive Risk Mitigation** (if conditionally approved):
- [ ] Third-party labor practice audit (conducted by recognized auditor) within 90 days
- [ ] Audit findings and remediation plan required within 120 days
- [ ] Quarterly compliance monitoring and reporting
- [ ] Cap on spend ($X limit until risk profile improves)
- [ ] Annual re-assessment (vs. 24 months for LOW risk)
- [ ] Right to terminate without cause if labor violations discovered

**Example:** Supplier with $500M revenue manufacturing in high-risk country with weak governance but strong business case. Conditional approval with 3rd-party audit required.

## 6. STATEMENT ACCEPTABILITY CRITERIA

**If Supplier Provides Modern Slavery Statement:**

### Minimum Acceptable (Pass)
- [ ] Statement is current (within 2 years)
- [ ] Covers forced labor, child labor, debt bondage, trafficking
- [ ] Identifies supply chain risks (not just operational risks)
- [ ] Describes remediation process or grievance mechanism
- [ ] Signed by responsible officer

### Preferred (Enhanced)
- [ ] Beyond-minimum features:
  - Identifies specific high-risk countries/industries
  - Names major supply chain partners
  - Describes labor practice audits or certifications
  - Includes quantified commitments (e.g., "Audit 100% of suppliers by 2027")
  - Demonstrates continuous improvement (comparison with prior year)

### Inadequate (Fail)
- [ ] Generic statement with no supplier-specific content
- [ ] Does not address supply chain (only own operations)
- [ ] No identification of risks
- [ ] No remediation process described
- [ ] Multiple years old (>2 years)

**Inadequate statements trigger:**
1. Request supplier to provide adequate statement within 30 days
2. If not provided: Escalate assessment to HIGH risk or REJECT

---

## 7. ESCALATION CRITERIA

**Escalate to Compliance Manager if ANY of:**
- [ ] Modern Slavery Statement required but not provided
- [ ] Statement provided but inadequate
- [ ] Risk score ≥ 7 points
- [ ] Supplier operates in sanctioned countries
- [ ] Prior findings of labor violations or trafficking
- [ ] Supplier is in labor-intensive industry (manufacturing, agriculture) with weak governance

**Escalate to Executive (CFO/COO) if:**
- [ ] HIGH risk assessment but strong business case for engagement
- [ ] Supplier is in defined high-risk industry AND high-risk country
- [ ] Cost of alternative supplier is >50% more expensive

---

## 8. ONGOING MONITORING

### Monitoring Schedule
| Risk Level | Frequency | Method |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| LOW | Every 24 months | Statement review + risk questionnaire |
| MEDIUM | Every 12 months | Statement review + risk questionnaire + audit of 1 condition |
| HIGH | Every 3 months | Compliance reporting + ongoing engagement |

### Trigger Events (Immediate Re-Assessment)
- [ ] Supplier appears in news related to labor violations
- [ ] Change in ownership/control (>50%)
- [ ] Change in primary supply chain (to high-risk country)
- [ ] Supplier fails to provide required statement on request
- [ ] Third-party audit identifies labor violations
- [ ] Whistleblower complaint related to labor practices

---

## 9. REMEDIATION & CORRECTIVE ACTION

**If supplier fails to meet conditions:**

1. **Warning** — Notify supplier of non-compliance (30 days to respond)
2. **Remediation Plan** — Supplier must submit corrective action plan within 30 days
3. **Monitoring** — Increase monitoring frequency, require monthly reporting
4. **Escalation** — If not remediated within 60 days, escalate to Compliance Manager
5. **Termination** — If not resolved within 90 days, terminate supplier contract

---

## 10. DOCUMENTATION & RECORD KEEPING

Document all assessments with:
- [ ] Assessment date
- [ ] Risk factors evaluated
- [ ] Risk score and classification
- [ ] Statement review (if applicable)
- [ ] Decision (APPROVED / CONDITIONAL / ESCALATED / REJECTED)
- [ ] Conditions (if applicable)
- [ ] Analyst name and approval authority
- [ ] Review schedule (next assessment date)

**Retention:** Maintain for 7 years
