# Insurance Requirements Standard

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Owner:** Procurement & Risk  

---

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines minimum insurance requirements for suppliers based on category and service type. It ensures suppliers maintain adequate coverage and financial accountability.

---

## 2. Coverage Requirements by Supplier Category

### Standard Supplier (Annual spend <$1M)

**Public Liability Insurance**
- Minimum cover: **$10 million** per claim
- Acceptable issuers: Licensed Australian insurers or international equivalent (rated A- or better)
- Scope: Must cover bodily injury and property damage arising from supplier operations

**No other insurance required** unless specifically applicable to services.

---

### High-Value Supplier (Annual spend $1M–$10M)

**Public Liability Insurance**
- Minimum cover: **$20 million** per claim
- Scope: As above

**Professional Indemnity Insurance** (if applicable)
- Minimum cover: **$5 million** per claim
- Applicable to: Service providers, consultants, IT providers, engineers, designers
- Exception: Service providers <$1M annual spend may use $2M minimum

**Directors & Officers Liability** (optional but recommended)
- Minimum cover: **$5 million**
- For suppliers with significant governance risk

---

### Strategic Supplier (Annual spend >$10M)

**Public Liability Insurance**
- Minimum cover: **$50 million** per claim (or higher based on industry)

**Professional Indemnity Insurance**
- Minimum cover: **$10 million** per claim (where applicable)

**Directors & Officers Liability**
- Minimum cover: **$10 million**

**Management Liability**
- Covers employment practices, cyber liability, statutory liability
- Minimum cover: **$5 million** (cyber component minimum $2M)

**Overseas Suppliers:** Additional coverage for political risk, war, terrorism (as applicable)

---

## 3. Certificate of Currency Requirements

A Certificate of Currency (CoC) is the proof of active insurance. It must:

- ✅ Be dated within the last **30 days**
- ✅ Show the **policy number** and expiry date
- ✅ State the **coverage amounts** for each policy
- ✅ Be issued by the **insurer or authorized broker**
- ✅ Show **no exclusions or conditions** that limit coverage

### Format
CoC can be provided as:
- Original pdf from insurer
- Email from broker (must show insurer details)
- Attachment to confirmation letter from insurance provider

### Not Acceptable
- ❌ Quotation or proposal (not active coverage)
- ❌ Policy schedule without CoC
- ❌ Screenshot of insurer portal (unverified)
- ❌ Supplier attestation ("We have coverage") — must have evidence

---

## 4. Expiry Tolerance

### Current Tolerance
- CoC is considered **current** if expiry date is ≥30 days in the future
- Example: If today is Jan 15, insurance expiring Feb 20 is acceptable (36 days)

### Renewal Management
- **Warning threshold:** Insurance expires within 30 days → Request renewal
- **Block threshold:** Insurance already expired → Require new CoC before proceeding
- **Acceptable lag:** Supplier may provide updated CoC dated within 7 days of old expiry

### Renewal Process
When insurance expires:
1. Case enters "Insurance Renewal Pending" status (not blocked, but flagged)
2. Procurement sends reminder at day -30
3. Supplier provides updated CoC
4. Procurement updates case file and confirms coverage

---

## 5. Specific Coverage Areas

### Public Liability

**Covers:**
- Bodily injury to third parties (employees, contractors, public)
- Property damage (damage to third-party assets)
- Legal defense costs

**Excludes:**
- Professional errors (covered by Professional Indemnity instead)
- Employment-related injuries (covered by Workers Compensation)
- Cyber/data breaches

**How Phase 5 validates:**
- Extract policy type: "Public Liability" or "General Liability"
- Extract minimum cover amount
- Verify meets requirement: $10M / $20M / $50M
- Extract expiry date
- Compare to today + 30 days

---

### Professional Indemnity

**Covers:**
- Errors or omissions in professional services
- Advice-related claims
- Design/engineering defects

**Required for:**
- IT service providers
- Management consultants
- Engineers and designers
- Financial advisors
- Architects

**Not required for:**
- Product manufacturers (covered by PLI)
- Labor-only contractors
- Commodity suppliers

**How Phase 5 validates:**
- Extract policy type: "Professional Indemnity"
- Verify applicable to supplier services
- Extract coverage amount: $2M / $5M / $10M
- Extract expiry date

---

### Directors & Officers Liability

**Covers:**
- Claims against company directors/officers for alleged wrongful acts
- Employment practices liability
- Statutory liability fines

**Optional but recommended for:**
- Companies with complex governance
- High-risk industries (financial services, import/export)
- Any company >50 employees

---

### Cyber/Management Liability

**Covers:**
- Data breach costs (notification, credit monitoring, PR)
- Ransomware and extortion
- Business interruption (cyber events)
- Compliance fines (privacy law violations)

**Minimum for strategic suppliers:** $2M cyber component

---

## 6. Overseas Suppliers

For suppliers operating outside Australia:

| Location | Additional Requirements |
|----------|------------------------|
| **USA/Canada/UK/EU** | Standard requirements (local insurer equivalent) |
| **High-risk jurisdiction** (Iran, North Korea, Syria, Russia) | NOT PERMITTED (sanctions) |
| **Middle East** | Political risk + war exclusion review |
| **Asia-Pacific** | Standard + currency hedging discussion |

---

## 7. Acceptable Insurance Issuers

### Acceptable
- ✅ Licensed Australian insurers (ASIC registration)
- ✅ Major international insurers (A.M. Best rating ≥A-)
- ✅ Insurance brokers issuing on behalf of rated insurers
- ✅ Industry mutual insurance schemes

### Unacceptable
- ❌ Overseas insurers without A- or better rating
- ❌ Captive insurance (supplier owns insurer)
- ❌ Self-insurance or retention-only policies

---

## 8. Policy Exclusions

**Red Flags** — Request clarification if CoC contains:

| Exclusion | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| "Excludes pollution" | Acceptable unless supplier handles pollutants |
| "Excludes professional advice" | Must have separate Professional Indemnity |
| "Geographic limit: USA only" | May not cover Australian operations |
| "Excludes contractual liability" | Request confirmation of coverage under our agreements |
| "Aggregate limit" | Confirm sufficient for multi-claim year |

---

## 9. Claims History

**Optional:** Request disclosure of:
- Recent claims (last 5 years)
- Claims under the current policy
- Claims history with insurer

**High-risk scenario:**
- Multiple claims in recent years → May request higher cover or security deposit

---

## 10. Change of Circumstances

Suppliers must notify Procurement if:

- Insurance policy is changed or reduced
- Insurer becomes insolvent
- Material claim is made
- Supplier changes ownership or structure
- Rated service significantly changes

**Notification:** Within 5 business days

---

## 11. Quarterly Verification

Procurement conducts quarterly compliance checks:

1. **System review:** CoC expiry dates in all active cases
2. **60-day warning:** Send reminder if <60 days to expiry
3. **30-day warning:** Send urgent request if <30 days to expiry
4. **Overdue:** Block case if insurance has expired

---

## 12. Acceptable Variations

### By Industry
- Manufacturing: PLI minimum $10M (higher for chemical/hazardous)
- IT services: PI minimum $5M (higher for financial IT)
- Labor: PLI minimum $5M (lower for skilled trades)

### By Geography
- Overseas: Equivalent local insurance acceptable
- Remote work: Standard coverage applies

### By Organizational Size
- Micro (<10 employees): May reduce Professional Indemnity to $2M
- Large (>1000 employees): May require self-insurance with security deposit

---

## 13. Self-Insurance Exception

Only approved for suppliers with:
- Net worth ≥ $500M
- A+ credit rating
- Board approval
- Security deposit equal to 150% of minimum required cover

**Approval authority:** Chief Financial Officer + Compliance Officer
